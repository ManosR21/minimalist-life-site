Digital Decluttering: Simplify Your Online Life

Minimalism isn’t only physical. The digital world around us is crowded with notifications, emails, and endless scrolling. Digital decluttering is about reclaiming time and focus by cleaning your online space.

Unsubscribe from newsletters you never read. Delete unused apps. Organize your desktop and cloud folders. Turn off notifications that steal your attention. Every deleted file is mental space regained.
